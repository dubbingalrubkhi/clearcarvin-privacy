Delete Your ClearCap Account

To request deletion of your ClearCap account and all associated data, send an email to:

support@clearcarvin.com

Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your translation history and Pro session data
  • Your credit balance and payment records

What is retained

  • Transaction records required for tax and legal compliance (retained for 7 years per US law)
