Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ClearCap ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ClearCap app.

Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you create an account or sign in with Google, used to identify your account and send verification emails.
  • Videos you upload — processed on our servers to generate translated subtitles, then deleted after processing is complete. We do not retain your videos.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details.
  • Google account information — if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and Google account ID. We do not receive your password.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process your video translation requests
  • To send account verification and password reset emails
  • To process payments via Stripe

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Anthropic — subtitle text is sent to Anthropic's API for Pro mode translation. No personal account information is shared.
  • Google — if you use Google sign-in
  • Resend — for sending transactional emails

Data Retention

Uploaded videos are deleted after processing. Account data is retained until you delete your account. We do not currently offer in-app account deletion — contact us at the email below to request deletion.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and secure token storage.

Children's Privacy

ClearCap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact us at: support@clearcarvin.com
